Why Messi gets the nod

He doesn't just finish attacks; he builds them. The dribbling, vision, passing, and scoring all come from the same left foot, letting him shape a game even when he isn't the one taking the shot. Add the World Cup, two Copa Américas, and a record eight Ballon d'Ors, and his all-around case is extraordinary.

And Ronaldo?

A phenomenal goalscorer, athlete, and Champions League force, with a remarkable international career of his own. The debate is about what you value most. For orchestration and all-round influence, we're wearing the number 10 shirt.
